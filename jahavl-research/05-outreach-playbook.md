## 1. Strategic rationale (answer the three questions)

### Q1 — What does the revamp + mobile actually deliver for him?

Map features → **business outcomes**, not feature lists.

| What ships | Mechanism | Outcome for JAH (if he still sells gear) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Series catalog + product detail (LED / lighting / audio) | Buyers and dealers see the line without emailing Jordan for a PDF dump | **Acquisition friction ↓** — fewer “send me your catalog” loops |
| Role-aware quoting (client / dealer / sales / admin) + dealer price / markup | Quotes move through a channel graph instead of a single inbox | **Conversion ↑** on mid/high-ticket cycles (financing, multi-SKU LED walls) where delay kills deals |
| Quote → ticket / message / live update path | Post-sale and mid-sale exceptions stay in one system | **Operational leverage** — CEO not personally chasing every status thread |
| Mobile companion (Expo, role sessions) | Sales/dealers act from the field without VPN theater | **Repeat / channel leverage** — dealers can re-quote and service without flying everything through Fort Pierce |
| Admin catalog control | One source of truth for list vs dealer economics | **Margin discipline** — less spreadsheet drift |

**Honest ceiling:** This is **not** a growth-hacking acquisition engine. Public third-party presence for JAH is thin; he is not obviously buying ads or SEO for the AV brand. The revamp’s real job is **selling-system reliability and CEO/channel bandwidth** — the same class of problem he argues about for churches (“one OS,” “Sunday never moves,” don’t vibe-code high-stakes systems).

If we can only say “nicer website,” **do not pitch**. If we can say “your AV business still has a multi-role quote surface that behaves like a brittle inbox, and we already modeled the OS for it,” **pitch**.

### Q2 — Is this worth his time and capital?

**Facts that constrain ROI:**

- Active FL corp since 2017; Hardison is RA + CEO; officer search also ties him to AIO.CHURCH LLC and Redefine Church Corp.
- Directory / aggregator footprint implies **minimal headcount** — treat JAH as tightly held, founder-operated.
- His **published** bandwidth is dominated by AIO / agents / AIO Assist — not JAH go-to-market theater.
- `jahavl.com` **does** expose a manufacturer catalog (series pages, some list prices). What is sparse is **press, directories, and proof of scaled commerce ops** — not the existence of a site. Do not open with “you have no catalog.”

**Proportionate scope (go):**

- Frame capital as **cutover / adoption of an already-built selling OS** (web + API + mobile + roles), not a greenfield “app project” that burns six figures before value.
- Narrow economic story: **hours of founder attention per quote cycle** and **dealer self-serve** beat “brand redesign.”
- Price and SOW must be milestone-sized (cutover → dealer net → PDF/pay) — not retainer fog.

**Disproportionate scope (no-go):**

- Pitching a multi-year platform build, custom native apps from zero, or “we’ll grow JAH like a DTC brand.”
- Asking him to fund speculative acquisition marketing while AIO is the company he is publicly building.

**Verdict:** Worth a **low-friction CEO reply** if positioned as a **bounded OS test case on his own AV selling surface**. Not worth a capital pitch framed as a vanity mobile app.

| Rank | Focus (public evidence) | Implication for copy |

| 1 | **AI operating systems** for churches / builders (AIO.Church, R.ai, AIO Assist) | Entry point must be systems / agents / reliability language |

**Thesis for outreach:** Lead with the systems thinker. Position the revamp as a **concrete application of the OS discipline he already evangelizes** — applied to JAH’s channel/quote surface — so that ignoring the note means ignoring a peer who did homework on *both* sides of his life.

## 2. Multi-touch sequence (5 touches)

**Design rules**

- Touch 1 goal = **a reply**, not a meeting.
- Open with **account signal**, never “we are / we built.”
- Include a deliberate **why this may not be worth it** line (credibility device).
- CTA = reaction to a **narrow question**, not “book a demo.”
- Channels: LinkedIn primary; email secondary (support@jahavl.com or known address). Soft YouTube only as optional warm-up — never the pitch.

**Timing**

| Day | Touch | Channel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | T1 | LinkedIn DM |
| 3 | T2 | Email (if no reply) |
| 7 | T3 | LinkedIn comment *or* short DM bump (one observation, no attach dump) |
| 12 | T4 | Email #2 — kill-switch honesty + optional one-pager |
| 18 | T5 | Final LinkedIn close-the-loop (permission to stop) |

Stop after any reply. Stop after T5 regardless.

## 3. Kill criteria (when *not* to pursue)

Execute the sequence only if none of the following are true. If any become true mid-sequence, **stop**.

| Kill if… | Why |
| --- | --- |
| K1 | We cannot name a concrete outcome beyond “modern site/app” | He will classify us as vendors |
| K2 | The commercial ask requires six-figure greenfield before any cutover value | Disproportionate to a tightly held AV entity with sparse growth signals |
| K3 | He (or a delegate) states JAH is **paused / legacy / not selling** | No ROI surface |
| K4 | He asks for AIO/ChMS/agent work as the engagement | Out of lane — politely decline; do not morph the pitch |
| K5 | We accidentally frame him as JH Audio / Jerry Harvey | Credibility dead on arrival |
| K6 | First touch opens with our bio, our stack flex, or “saw your website could use a refresh” | Template tell; rewrite before send |
| K7 | CTA is “jump on a 30–45 min demo” as the first ask | Wrong altitude for this CEO; reply first |
| K8 | We imply the PDF/revamp replaces his judgment on capital allocation | He builds OS products; he will reject captured narratives |

**Pre-send checklist (60 seconds)**

- [ ] Sounds like a person (contractions, no numbered “framework,” no consultant voice)  
- [ ] **Staging URL + at least one login** in T1 (and T2) — never “we built something” without keys  
- [ ] Mobile mentioned as real companion on same API (offer build link, don’t fake a store listing)  
- [ ] Soft “if AIO owns the year, ignore” line present  
- [ ] Entity disambiguation once (JAH ≠ JH Audio)  
- [ ] Ask is a reaction / click, not “book a demo”  
- [ ] Kill criteria K1–K8 reviewed  
- [ ] Confirm `https://jahavl.hivelyhr.com` and demo logins still work before send

## 4. If he replies — escalation (only after reply)

Do **not** put this in the cold sequence. Use only when he engages.

1. **Reply hygiene:** Mirror his framing; answer the question he asked; offer one artifact (staging URL *or* one-pager), not both dumped at once.  
2. **15-minute frame (if he offers time):** One question only — “Where does founder attention still sit in the quote → dealer → close path?” Then show the matching screen.  
3. **Commercial shape:** Cutover milestone, not retainer. Dealer net / PDF / payments as optional second milestone.  
4. **Walk away script:** “If AIO owns the year, we should not force a JAH workstream. Happy to reconnect when the selling OS is the bottleneck again.”
