## The opportunity

JAH Audio designs and manufactures LED walls, stage lighting, and live sound systems for churches, venues, and touring productions. The product line is sophisticated; the public selling surface should match that sophistication — and the reliability bar you set for Sunday operations.

We rebuilt a production-ready **web + mobile** experience around how JAH actually sells: series-level catalog, multi-role quoting, and live operational updates.
