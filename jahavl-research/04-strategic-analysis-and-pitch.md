# Strategic Analysis & Pitch Positioning

## Who we are talking to

Jordan Hardison is not a passive “church AV owner who needs a website.” He is:

1. **CEO of a real product company** (JAH Audio since 2017) selling LED / lighting / audio into worship and venues.
2. **Founder of an AI-native church OS** (AIO.Church + R.ai) and an agentic builder workspace (AIO Assist).
3. **Lead pastor / nonprofit officer** (Redefine Church) — dogfoods his own stack.
4. **Technically fluent** — multi-model workflows, agent governance, live coding on YouTube, opinions on agency pricing in 2026.

Any pitch that sounds like a junior agency discovery call will lose him in the first paragraph.

## What “openness to a business pitch” looks like

| Signal | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Builds in public; accepts that software is unfinished | Open to **sharp operators**, not consultants who only workshop |
| Warns against vibe-coding ChMS | Will reject anyone who claims “we’ll AI your whole church stack” over AIO |
| Lists JAH Audio on LinkedIn but leads with AI | JAH digital ops may be **under-invested relative to AIO** — opportunity, if framed as freeing him to focus on OS products |
| Cares about Sunday reliability | JAH quote/catalog bugs = lost ministry deals; speak reliability language |
| Mentions C++ audio engines + AI coding | Respect engineering depth; don’t dumb down AV product talk |
| Small public network, high-signal posts | Prefer **precise LinkedIn DM / warm email** over mass outreach |

**Verdict:** Open to a pitch that is (a) specific to **JAH commerce + channel software**, (b) technically literate, (c) non-competitive with AIO, (d) evidence-backed from work already done.

## How we contribute best output

### Primary offer (highest fit)

**JAH Audio digital commerce & channel platform** — modern public catalog, quote/PDF pipeline, dealer/client/sales/admin roles, live updates, mobile companion — i.e. the `jaha_live_revamp` direction.

Why it fits:

- Matches product catalog complexity (LED wall planning, series, pricing, financing narrative).
- Matches multi-role B2B sales (client / dealer / sales / admin) already modeled in the revamp.
- Lets Hardison keep AIO as the AI OS story while JAH gets a professional selling system.
- Aligns with his “operating system” thinking applied to **his own AV business ops**.

### Secondary offers (only after trust)

- Performance, QA, and release engineering for JAH web/mobile.
- Integration bridges (e.g. quote events → CRM/email) **without** rebuilding ChMS.
- Design systems that feel “OS-grade” (calm, reliable, volunteer-usable metaphors).

### Offers to avoid leading with

- Replacing AIO.Church / R.ai / AIO Assist.
- Generic SEO or social media retainers.
- “We’ll train your pastors on ChatGPT.”
- Confusing JAH with JH Audio prestige narrative.

## Pitch narrative (recommended)

**Title idea:** *A selling OS for JAH — so the AV business runs as cleanly as your Sunday stack.*

**Arc:**

1. **Recognition** — We know you’re building AI operating systems for churches *and* running JAH Audio; we won’t conflate you with Jerry Harvey’s JH Audio.
2. **Evidence** — We’ve already rebuilt a modern JAH-facing web + mobile experience (catalog, quotes, roles, live updates) against a faithful domain model.
3. **Gap** — Legacy jahavl.com still carries catalog/CRM friction; channel partners need a trustworthy quote surface.
4. **Ask** — A short technical walkthrough with you (or your delegate): production path for the revamp, priorities (mobile, dealer net, Stripe, PDF, approvals), and how we stay out of AIO’s lane.
5. **Tone** — Peer builder to peer builder; short; no buzzword soup.
