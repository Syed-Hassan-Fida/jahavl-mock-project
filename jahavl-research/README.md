## One-line thesis

Hardison is a **builder-operator**: he runs a worship/venue AV brand *and* ships AI operating systems for churches and builders. Generic agency pitches will fail. Credible outreach must show respect for (1) JAH’s product/catalog commerce needs and (2) his technical fluency on agents, reliability, and “operating system” products — without pretending we replace AIO.Church.
