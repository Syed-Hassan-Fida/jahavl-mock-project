Outreach, proposals, and verbal pitches **must** stay anchored to the correct legal entity and person.

## Why confusion happens

- Similar letters (JAH vs JH).
- Both Florida-adjacent pro-audio brands.
- Both sell into live performance ecosystems.
- Search engines and non-specialists often autocomplete Jerry Harvey first.

## Outreach rule

Never say “JH Audio,” “Jerry Harvey,” “custom IEMs,” or “Sound Devices acquisition” when talking to Hardison about **his** company. If asked about name collision, acknowledge briefly and move on:

> “We’re speaking with you about JAH Audio Corp. on jahavl.com — LED, lighting, and live sound for worship and venues — not the Jerry Harvey IEM company.”
